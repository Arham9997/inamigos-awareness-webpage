Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation (IAF) is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. To ensure transparency and accountability, InAmigos Foundation holds recognitions such as 80G & 12A Certification, CSR-1 Registration, NGO Darpan Registration, and ISO 9001:2015 Certification.Guided by the motto "Uniting Minds for Change," the foundation works across India to promote education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, humanitarian support, and community development.

Our Key Initiatives: Project SEVA - Humanitarian Support & Community Welfare, Project Bachpanshala - Education for Underprivileged Children, Project UDAAN - Women Empowerment & Skill Development, Project PRAKRITI - Environmental Conservation & Sustainability, Project JEEV - Animal Welfare & Care, and Project VIKAS - Community Development & Social Progress.

The organization has built a strong network of volunteers, interns, professionals, and supporters who actively contribute to social causes. Through active community engagement and social media outreach, the foundation raises awareness and builds meaningful partnerships to support its mission. Join us in creating meaningful change and making a lasting difference in communities across India.
